pcm.v2 models were generated from pcm.v1 by the script `Code/modelUpdates/updateToV2.m`.

`pcm.noConstNoLight.v2.xml` was generated by removing both pseudo-metabolites that are not retained in reactions, and light from `pcm.v2.xml` to show that the model has, aside from these metabolites, stoichiometric consistency (see the MEMOTE report in `Data/analysis/MEMOTE/`).
